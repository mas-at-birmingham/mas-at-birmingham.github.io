## What We're Aiming For

We want a balance: each session should either **spark ideas** or **help you build skills**.

**Interesting:** a window into the range of multi-agent work going on
* Research talks from members and invited speakers
* Panel discussions and debates
* Reading groups

**Useful:** support and upskilling at different stages of research
* **Earlier in the PhD:** research pitches with friendly feedback, talking through whether an idea is viable, and how to pivot when things don't work out
* **Later PhD and staff:** grant and joint proposal writing, rebuttals, quick-turnaround workshop papers, finding collaborators, and staff research pitches

We may also write up some discussions as short blog posts.
