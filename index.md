# Multi-Agent Systems @ Birmingham (MAS@B)

Welcome to the **MAS@B** group! We are an interdisciplinary research community at the University of Birmingham focusing on multi-agent systems, cooperative AI, game theory, distributed consensus, and autonomous agent coordination.

## Meeting Schedule
We meet fortnightly on **Thursdays at 14:00** (Lab / Hybrid).
