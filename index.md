Welcome to **MAS@B**! We are an interdisciplinary interest group spanning research themes across the School of Computer Science at the University of Birmingham. 

Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) is fundamentally cross-cutting. We bring together researchers, faculty, and PhD students working on theoretical foundations, algorithmic frameworks, hardware deployments, and emerging AI models where autonomous entities interact.

## Broad Research Themes
* **MARL:** Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning, Human-Agent Teaming, communication protocols.
* **Robotics & Swarms:** Multi-robot coordination, swarm systems, multi-agent pathfinding (MAPF).
* **MAS for IoT, Tiny Devices & Edge AI:** Resource-constrained multi-agent execution, TinyML, dynamic sensor networks, decentralized edge computing.
* **Game Theory & Mechanism Design:** Strategic decision-making, dynamic games, auction design, fair resource allocation.
* **Agentic AI & LLM Frameworks:** Multi-agent LLM systems, autonomous workflows, tool-use orchestration.

## What We Do
* **Informal Knowledge Exchange:** Discussions to share work-in-progress, test early-stage ideas, and troubleshoot research roadblocks in a relaxed environment.
* **Student & Researcher Tutorials:** Hands-on deep dives led by PhD students and postdocs to cross-develop technical skills across labs.
* **Cross-Group Funding & Proposals:** Identifying grant opportunities and assembling joint research bids.
* **Workshops & Event Signposting:** Organizing internal one-day workshops and cross-promoting MAS-related seminars across campus.
