Welcome to **MAS@B**! We are an interdisciplinary interest group spanning research themes across the School of Computer Science at the University of Birmingham.

At MAS@B, agents can be anything from RL learners and robot swarms to IoT devices and LLMs. Everyone's welcome: you don't need any multi-agent background, and if you work with agents of any kind, you'll fit right in.

**Next session:** Thursday 19 November 2026, 11:00, LG23 (or on Teams). [See the schedule](/schedule).

Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) is fundamentally cross-cutting. We bring together researchers, faculty, and PhD students working on theoretical foundations, algorithmic frameworks, hardware deployments, and emerging AI models where autonomous entities interact.

## Broad Research Themes
* **MARL:** Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning, Human-Agent Teaming, communication protocols.
* **Robotics & Swarms:** Multi-robot coordination, swarm systems, multi-agent pathfinding (MAPF).
* **MAS for IoT, Tiny Devices & Edge AI:** Resource-constrained multi-agent execution, TinyML, dynamic sensor networks, decentralized edge computing.
* **Game Theory & Mechanism Design:** Strategic decision-making, dynamic games, auction design, fair resource allocation.
* **Agentic AI & LLM Frameworks:** Multi-agent LLM systems, autonomous workflows, tool-use orchestration.

## What We Do
We aim for a mix of sessions that are interesting and sessions that are genuinely useful. See the [tentative plan](/schedule#looking-ahead-tentative).

* **Talks & Discussions:** Research talks, invited speakers, panels and reading groups across the many kinds of multi-agent work.
* **Research Support & Feedback:** Pitch ideas, get feedback, and talk through what to do when a project needs to pivot.
* **Upskilling:** Tutorials, plus practical sessions on grant writing, joint proposals, rebuttals and workshop papers.
* **Collaboration & Funding:** Finding collaborators and funding calls, and putting together joint bids.
* **Workshops & Event Signposting:** Internal workshops, and cross-promoting MAS-related seminars across campus.
